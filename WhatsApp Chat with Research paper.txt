OOP Purity generally refers to how strictly a language enforces or embodies the core OOP principles. The classic four are:
PrincipleWhat it means for purity measurementEncapsulationCan data be truly hidden? Are access modifiers enforced?InheritanceIs inheritance supported cleanly? Python is multi-paradigm — OOP is optional, not enforced
C++ supports OOP but is not OOP-first
Ruby is closer to Smalltalk — everything is an object

Your framework needs to quantify these differences systematically.
